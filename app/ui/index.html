Phone

Alerts can be pushed to your phone through ntfy, which is free and needs no account. Put a topic here, subscribe to the same topic in the ntfy app, and that is the whole setup.

The topic name is the only thing protecting it. There is no password, so anyone who knows the name can read your alerts and send you fake ones. Use the generated one rather than your own name, and keep anything private out of alert text.

Browser

Alerts from Claude in your browser, in the same voice as the ones from your terminal. This also lets the browser tell your machine when your usage window resets, so the countdown stays right even on a day you never open Claude Code.

Claude Code Sounds

Version —

A sound when Claude finishes, when it needs you, and when your usage limit resets. It is meant to let you look away from the terminal without losing your place.

How it stays cheap

The usage figures are ones Claude already hands your machine. Reading them runs no model, so nothing here can use up the limit it reports, and no tokens are ever spent.

What leaves your machine

Nothing, unless you switch it on. There is no analytics and no telemetry. Phone alerts are off by default and go only to the server you name.
